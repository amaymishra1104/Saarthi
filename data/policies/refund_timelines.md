# Policy POL-RFT-004: Refund and Reversal Timelines

## Summary
Defines standard Turnaround Time (TAT) by payment rail, banking reference requirements, and handling of premature customer queries.

## Policy Rules
1. **Rule RFT-1: Payment Rail Turnaround Times (TAT)**
   - **UPI / IMPS:** Reversals settle within 24 to 48 banking hours.
   - **Debit / Credit Cards:** Reversals settle within 3 to 5 business days depending on the card issuer network.
   - **Net Banking:** Reversals take 2 to 4 business days.
   - **Stored Value Wallet:** Reversals settle immediately or within 2 hours.

2. **Rule RFT-2: Disclosure of Banking Reference**
   Whenever a reversal is verified and confirmed, the agent must provide the customer with the Bank Reference Number (UTR / RRN) generated by the clearing switch.

3. **Rule RFT-3: Handling In-Window Inquiries**
   If a reversal is already in `REVERSAL_PENDING` or `REVERSED` status and the elapsed time is within the standard TAT, the system must NOT initiate a new action. Instead, provide the existing reference number and expected credit date.
